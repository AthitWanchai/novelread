## Findings

25. **LOW - R-20:** The approved hero uses a purpose-composed panoramic illustration. Current slides use each novel's portrait cover cropped with `object-fit: cover` to fill the panoramic frame. The crop may remove the cover's focal subject; source inspection cannot establish how severe it looks with the current covers. Evidence: `web/components/novel/spotlight-carousel.tsx`, `.spotlight-art img` in `web/app/globals.css`, and `design/references/home-approved-c.png`.
